Zone status

Each borough is compared with its own normal: 12 months of real FDNY incidents (NYC Open Data). 🟢 Normal · 🟡 Watch (more out-of-limit calls than chance, or p90 above normal with 95% confidence) · 🔴 Needs attention (sustained shift, many calls above the limit, or p90 above normal with 99% confidence).     Log scale. The box is the middle half of the calls, the line is the median, and a dot beyond the whisker is a call past Tukey's fence (1.5 x the middle half). It describes this zone; it never changes a status.

    When the slow calls happen — weekday × hour

    Each cell is the p90 of the calls in that weekday and hour, in minutes. A cell with fewer than 20 calls is grey and marked "·": that means not enough evidence, never a fast response.
